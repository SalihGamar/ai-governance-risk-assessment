# AI Risk Framework

## 1. Data Sensitivity
Evaluates the sensitivity of data used by the AI system.
Higher risk if personal, financial, or confidential data is involved.

## 2. Bias & Fairness
Assesses potential for discriminatory or unfair outcomes.
Higher risk in HR, lending, or customer decision systems.

## 3. Explainability
Measures how easily AI decisions can be understood.
Lower explainability increases governance risk.

## 4. Operational Dependency
Evaluates how much the organization depends on the AI system.
Higher dependency increases impact if failure occurs.

## 5. Regulatory Exposure
Assesses legal and compliance risks related to AI usage.
Higher exposure in regulated industries or data environments.
