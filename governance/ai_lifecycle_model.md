# AI Lifecycle Governance Model

## 1. Proposed
AI use case identified but not yet built or deployed.

## 2. In Development
AI system currently being designed, trained, or tested.

## 3. Deployed
AI system active in production and impacting real users or operations.

## Governance Principle
Risk exposure increases after deployment due to real-world impact,
data drift, automation level, and operational dependency.

## Review Triggers
- Lifecycle stage changes
- Data scope changes
- Automation level increases
- Regulatory or compliance concerns
